# P3 (Regie docks): strings that E2E tests may assert

All user-visible texts of `apps/studio/src/app/docks/**`, as `old German -> new English` per catalogue key. The column
"E2E" is a heuristic ("yes" when the old German text, up to its first placeholder, occurs in `tests/e2e`); P7 should
still grep for the German text. Placeholders are `{name}`; plural messages are shown with their `other` form. The
German column is unchanged in `packages/i18n/src/messages/de-CH/`.

Points worth knowing when converting the specs:

- Visible structure is unchanged, only the words differ. Numbers go through the format helpers: "2,8 Mbit/s" becomes
  "2.8 Mbit/s", "Akku 14 %" becomes "Battery 14%", "78 %" becomes "78%" in English.
- "Technik" (station details) is now "Tech check"; its lamps read "Camera ok", "Network ok", "Upload borderline 4.2 Mbit/s".
- Other-day pairing time: "21.10. 09:05" becomes "21/10 09:05" in English (day/month order is part of the message).
- The close button of the pairing dock is "Close pair device" (was "Gerät koppeln schliessen").
- Quick messages sent to a device are now in the language of the control room: "Pause" becomes "Break", "In 10 s live"
  becomes "Live in 10 s", "Bitte lächeln" becomes "Please smile".
- New scenes are named "New scene N", copies "Name (copy)" and "Name (copy 2)".
- The sentence that explains where a medium is used was rebuilt as "Used in “X” and as background of “Y”." (was
  "Wird in «X» und als Hintergrund von «Y» verwendet."); the label before it is "in use" (was "wird verwendet").
- "wieder da / war 9 s weg" is "back / was gone 9 s"; the mono digits keep their own element.
- The phone tab hint is "Valid once, 29:59 min left." (was "Gilt einmal, noch 29:59 min.").
